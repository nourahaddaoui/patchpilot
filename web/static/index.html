PatchPilot

An agent that finds a known-vulnerable Python dependency, bumps its pin, runs the tests and rescans, and writes nothing until a human approves the exact change. Pick a scenario, start it, and be the human.

Demo: the scanner (Trivy), pip and pytest are simulated per scenario. The agent, its tools, the approval checkpoint, recovery and the stop rule are the real code.
